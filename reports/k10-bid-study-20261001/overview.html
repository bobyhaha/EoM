Interpretation

The primary study changes only objective wording and the bidding package. Follow-ups are exploratory and separately labeled. No task carries learned state into another. This is not a full 40-task trained-population comparison and not a held-out efficacy claim.

All team cells retain voluntary membership, equal per-member path rewards, and λ=0. The fixed bid requires explicit personal payment consent; dissenters are not charged. Model-generated grades are noisy; repeat grades remain separate from primary scores.
